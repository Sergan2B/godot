# Sprint 11 External Codex Beta usability study consent — v1

This is a local usability study of the Godot Codex External Beta workflow.
Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time without giving a reason.

The study records a pseudonymous run identifier, task and fault outcomes,
bounded timings, wrong-turn counts, help-source identifiers, comprehension
rubric outcomes, and content-free event digests. It does not record your name,
account identity, prompts, project source, property values, authentication
material, approval receipts, native handles, private absolute paths, or screen
recordings.

The operator will use a fresh disposable copy of the test fixture and the
frozen package identified before the run. Developer coaching and fixture
answers are prohibited. The operator may intervene only for consent, safe
fault injection or reset, or an immediate safety stop.

Your local subject key and the random study salt used to form the pseudonym
remain in a private consent ledger outside the repository and outside release
evidence. They are retained only until the stated withdrawal deadline and are
then destroyed. Only the salted SHA-256 pseudonym is placed in study records.

The operator must explain the retention period, withdrawal deadline, expected
duration, and the allowed help sources before asking for consent. Consent is
recorded as a content-free receipt bound to this exact document digest.

By explicitly agreeing, you confirm that you understand the collection above,
that you may stop, and that the resulting redacted pseudonymous records may be
used to qualify the local macOS arm64 External Codex Beta.
